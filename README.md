# 🌊 Atheryx : Campus Water Digital Twin
**By Team Armakine**

### 🎯 One-Line Problem Statement
Protecting critical infrastructure through a closed-loop digital twin that detects, predicts, and autonomously mitigates physical failures in real-time.

### 🏗️ The 4-Layer Architecture
We bridge the intelligence-action gap through a structured four-tiered ecosystem:
1. **Perception Layer:** ESP32 & Sensors (The Nerves).
2. **Transmission Layer:** MQTT & Mosquitto (The Spine).
3. **Multi-Agent Reasoning Layer:** A specialized AI squad—The Sentry, The Forecaster, and The Auditor (The Brain).
4. **Visualization Layer:** Three.js Digital Mirror (The Face).

### 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **AI Logic:** Gemini 2.0 Flash
- **IDE:** Kiro (Agentic Development)
- **Database:** InfluxDB (Open Source)

- **Communication:** Eclipse Mosquitto (MQTT)
